About Sami Zamat

A businessman shaped by trust and ambition.

Insurance & Risk Services

Sami Zamat is a Lebanese businessman, Insurance Agent and Insurance Consultant. Since 2021, he has built his career on one principle: protecting what matters most to people — their health, their families, their property and their businesses.

As the founder of Zamat Group — Insurance & Risk Services, he leads a growing practice between Beirut and Tyre, advising individuals, families and companies on the cover that truly fits them. His approach is personal and direct: clear advice, the right policy at the right price, and full follow-up on renewals, payments and claims.

His ambition is clear: to grow Zamat Insurance into a trusted name far beyond Lebanon — built on integrity, reliability, and relationships that last.
